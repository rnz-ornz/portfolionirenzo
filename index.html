About Me

As an Information Technology student, I find a unique kind of fulfillment in taking raw code HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and transforming it into functional, interactive websites from the ground up. There is a deeply satisfying feeling when a complex layout finally aligns perfectly with CSS Grid or when a JavaScript function executes smoothly to bring a page to life. My journey in web development is ongoing. I am constantly pushing myself to learn new technologies, refine my coding practices, and bridge the gap between theoretical IT concepts and practical, real-world applications. Whether I am structuring accessible semantic HTML, experimenting with modern styling techniques, or handling asynchronous operations in JavaScript, every project is a chance to experiment and grow.
